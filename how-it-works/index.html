How it works

One short call. Then your trade's news lands daily or weekly, under your name.

No software to learn and no password to remember. Geoff sets LongLens up with you, and from then on you change anything by replying to an issue.

Getting started

From a short call to your first issue

  1. Book a short call

    Tell Geoff who your customers are and what they should hear from you first. He reads every message himself and replies from geoff@wanglemedia.com.

    Email Geoff to book a call

  2. Geoff sets LongLens up with you

    He points LongLens at the websites, forums, videos and newsletters you trust, and you describe your customers in your own words. You see a week of real issues before anyone else does.

  3. Sign in with the link Geoff emails you

    Open it and press Get started. There is no password. Three short questions follow, on your own or with Geoff:

    • What should LongLens follow, said the way you would tell a friend?
    • Where and when: by email and on a web page, or the web page only, how often, what time, your time zone.
    • Where should LongLens start looking: websites, feeds or keywords, or a starter set.

  4. Your first issue can land today

    Press Make my newsletter. Then open Issues and press Make an issue now: LongLens starts on it within ten minutes and emails it to you.

Every morning

What lands in your inbox, and why you can trust it

At the time you picked, LongLens turns the day's news in your trade into one issue with your newsletter's name on it.

  1. LongLens catches anything new

    From every website, feed, forum and channel you follow.

  2. LongLens keeps what matters to your customers

    A story you have already had stays out, unless something changed. Then it comes back marked as an update.

  3. Every story says why it matters

    Most stories carry a short line on why they matter to your customers, and every story links to the original.

  4. Every link is tried first

    A story whose only link is dead is left out, and a line at the foot of the issue says how many links were checked and how many were dead.

  5. Your copy arrives by email

    Nothing to press. Every issue is also kept on the Issues page when you sign in.

  6. Audio, video and slides follow

    On the issue's page, once they pass their check. On extras days your copy can arrive up to a few hours later.

Your daily routine

Four answers cover most mornings

  • Change your issue: reply to any issue in plain words, for example "shorter please". The next issue follows it.
  • Undo a change: reply UNDO.
  • Pause it: press Pause this newsletter at the bottom of any issue. The same page starts it again.
  • Sign in: type your email on the sign-in page and a fresh link arrives by email, usually within a minute. Each link works once, and keeps you signed in for 30 days on that device.

Sending to your customers: today each issue comes to you first, and you send it through the email tool you already use, or forward it. coming Email straight to your customers from your own address.
