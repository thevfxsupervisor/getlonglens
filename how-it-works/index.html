Five quick moves keep every issue yours

  • Change a line: open the issue from the link in your email, press Review and approve, then Edit in place, click any line and type (daily newsletters; a weekly newsletter has its own editor).
  • Change the next issue: reply to the issue email and say what you want, for example "shorter please". LongLens emails back what it changed, and the next issue follows it.
  • Undo a change: reply with the one word UNDO.
  • Pause it: press Pause this newsletter at the bottom of any issue. The same page starts it again.
  • Sign in: type your email on the sign-in page and a fresh link arrives by email, usually within a minute. Each link works once, and keeps you signed in for 30 days on that device.
