Behind every issue

Why your clients can trust what they read from you

Not a pasted feed. In our own daily issues, about 1 in 20 stories makes the cut.

  1. Stop checking ten websites a day

    LongLens reads the websites, feeds and channels you pick, and brings you what matters.

  2. Each story is judged against what your readers care about

    Your replies, such as "more on X, less on Y", change what is chosen next time.

  3. The same story from two outlets appears once

    A story your readers already had stays out unless something changed. Then it comes back marked as an update, linked to the earlier coverage.

  4. Each story is written in LongLens's own words

    With a line on why it matters to your readers, not pasted from the original.

  5. Names, titles and numbers are checked against the original article

    Every story keeps a link to the original.

  6. Every link is checked, and dead links are dropped.

    A line at the foot of each issue says how many links were checked.

  7. A website that stops working or fills with junk is paused automatically

    You can see which one, and turn it back on with one tap.

  8. You read it before your readers do

    Change any line first. Add your existing clients with a single tick, and LongLens sends them each issue you sign off, with only your name on it and a one-click opt-out in every email.

  9. The Podcast, Reel and the rest follow on most issues

    The Email comes with every issue. Most issues also bring a Podcast, a Reel and an Infographic; some bring a Carousel or an Explainer video. Each appears on the issue's page when it is ready.

Day to day

Five quick moves keep every issue yours

  • Change a line: open the issue, press Edit in place, click any line and type (daily newsletters; a weekly newsletter has its own editor).
  • Change the next issue: reply in plain words, for example "shorter please". The next issue follows it.
  • Undo a change: reply UNDO.
  • Pause it: press Pause this newsletter at the bottom of any issue. The same page starts it again.
  • Sign in: type your email on the sign-in page and a fresh link arrives by email, usually within a minute. Each link works once, and keeps you signed in for 30 days on that device.
