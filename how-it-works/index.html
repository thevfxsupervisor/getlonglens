Every issue

What lands in your inbox, and why you can trust it

At the time you picked, LongLens turns the news in your field into one issue with your newsletter's name on it.

  1. LongLens catches anything new

    From every website, feed, forum and channel you follow.

  2. LongLens keeps what matters to your readers

    A story you have already had stays out, unless something changed. Then it comes back marked as an update.

  3. Every story says what it means for them

    Most stories carry a short line on why they matter to your readers, and every story links to the original.

  4. Links are checked first

    A story whose only link is dead is left out, and a line at the foot of the issue says how many links were checked.

  5. Your copy arrives by email

    Nothing to press. Every issue is also kept on the Issues page when you sign in.

  6. The other formats follow

    Audio, the picture and the videos appear on the issue's page once they pass their check, not on every issue.

Day to day

Five things cover most days

  • Change a line: open the issue, press Edit in place, click any line and type (daily newsletters; a weekly newsletter has its own editor).
  • Change the next issue: reply in plain words, for example "shorter please". The next issue follows it.
  • Undo a change: reply UNDO.
  • Pause it: press Pause this newsletter at the bottom of any issue. The same page starts it again.
  • Sign in: type your email on the sign-in page and a fresh link arrives by email, usually within a minute. Each link works once, and keeps you signed in for 30 days on that device.
