Your name, your words

Change any line right on the issue, exactly as your readers will see it

Open the issue, click the words you want to change, type, and press Save. The issue updates everywhere with your words, and every earlier version is kept. Or reply in plain words, "shorter" or "more on the new rules", and the next issue follows.

  • Your words on top: the headline, the opening, and the line people see in their inbox before they open it.
  • Your say on every story: the line on what it means for them, and each short summary.
  • The facts stay put: story titles and links stay as the original publisher wrote them, so every story still points to where it came from.
  • Your layout: put the sections in your order, hide the ones you do not need, and add a note from you.
