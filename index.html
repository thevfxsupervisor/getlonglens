Make it yours, on the page

Change any line right on your issue, exactly as your readers will see it.

No forms and no code. Open your issue, click the words you want to change, type, and press Save. LongLens rewrites the whole issue with your words in it and keeps every earlier version, so nothing you save is ever lost.

  • Your words on top: the headline, the opening, the line people see in their inbox before they open it.
  • Your say on every story: the line on what it means for them, and each short summary.
  • The facts stay put: story titles and links stay as the original publisher wrote them, so every story still points to its source.
  • Your sections, your order: drag the sections of a daily newsletter into the order you like, hide the ones you do not need, rename the headings and add a note from you. A preview shows your latest issue as you go.
