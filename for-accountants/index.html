Straight answers before you start

Can I pick sources from my own country?

Yes. Name the sources you trust, such as a tax authority, your professional body or the business press, and we set them up when you start. Sources with a news feed work when you paste them. Some government pages need us to set them up by hand, so tell us which ones matter most to you.

Whose name is on what my clients read, and will it look like my brand?

Yours, and only yours, so it looks like your firm, not a template. On every plan, every update carries your firm's name, logo and colours, never ours. Business adds a custom design of your letters, pages and videos, built with you when you start.

Who is responsible for record keeping and professional rules?

Your firm is. You decide what your clients are told, and you keep the records your own professional rules require. When LongLens sends for you, it keeps the proof of who was added as a client and who stopped.

What happens to my clients' details?

Your clients' names and email addresses are used for one thing: sending them your updates. They never go to an AI. Hit Reply on any update in your inbox and write what you want changed. Your replies shape your firm's updates and nobody else's, and your content is never used to teach an AI.
