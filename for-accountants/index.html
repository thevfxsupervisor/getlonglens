You choose the news, LongLens writes the issue

Name the pages and channels you trust. LongLens writes one short issue from them, with a line on what most stories mean for your clients and a link to every original.

A rule that changes stays one story

When a deadline moves or a rule is reversed, LongLens marks the story as an update or a reversal and links back to the earlier issues that covered it.

See what keeps coming up

Switch on "What keeps coming up" and, after a few issues, each issue can show what keeps coming up across your last few weeks of issues.
