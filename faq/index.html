Getting started

How soon can my clients start hearing from me?

Send the form and your first issue arrives by email in about 5 minutes, written from the sources and topics you gave. The next ones follow on weekdays at 07:00 your time, with nothing to press, and every issue ends with a Stop button. You can change what it covers and when on one page. If you never tap a link, reply or sign in for five issues, LongLens asks once whether you still want them and waits for your yes. On a very busy day you get a start link instead: click it and answer three short questions. You see a week of real issues before anyone else does. Today each issue comes to you first, and you send it through the email tool you already use, or forward it. Email straight to your readers from your own address is coming.

Can I sign up on my own?

Yes, from the form. Start your free week. Your first issue arrives by email in about 5 minutes.

How do I sign in?

With a link by email. There is no password. Type your email on the sign-in page and a fresh link arrives, usually within a minute. Each link works once and keeps you signed in for 30 days on that device.

Trust and control

Does anything reach my clients before I see it?

No. Nothing goes to your clients until you approve it. Every issue comes to you by email first: you read it, change any line, and decide what to send and to whom. If your issues also go up on a public page on your own website, each one waits for your OK there too, unless you choose a setting that puts it up for you: Automatic, or Publish at noon without my take (if you have not added your own comment by 12:00, it goes up without it). Both are off until you choose them, you are emailed when an issue goes up, and what goes up also reaches your Slack or Discord channel and your feed.

What if LongLens gets something wrong?

You read every issue before your readers do, and you can change or cut any line. LongLens checks names and figures against the original story and links every story to it, so each fact can be checked in one click. Your name is on it, so the last word is yours.

Whose name is on what my clients read, and will it look like my brand?

Yours, and only yours. Every issue carries your name, never ours. When you start, we match the look and feel to your brand.

Who is responsible for record keeping and professional rules?

Your firm is. You decide what your clients are told and you keep the records your own professional rules require. LongLens keeps the proof of who agreed to hear from you and who stopped.

Will AI-drafted issues sound like me?

AI drafts each issue, checks names and figures against the original story, and checks links before the issue goes out. You read and change every line first, on the page or by replying, so it sounds like you.

Can I make every line sound like me?

Yes. Open the issue, press Edit in place, click any line and type, then Save. Every version is kept. This works on daily newsletters; a weekly newsletter has its own editor, section by section.

Your clients' details

Does LongLens need my client list?

Not today. Issues come to you, and you send them from the email tool you already use, so your client list stays where it is.

Can LongLens follow news from my country?

Yes. LongLens follows the websites, feeds and forums you name, wherever they are: a tax authority's news page, an industry association, a regulator.

What you get

Is LongLens only for one kind of business?

No. LongLens works for any business whose readers need to know what is changing: an accountancy, a vet practice, a law firm, a recruiter, a consultancy, a school or a members' association. Geoff built it first for his own field, visual effects, and that is the example on this site. Yours follows the websites, forums and newsletters of your field.

What if the audio or videos are missing?

Not every issue gets every format. During the pilot, up to two of the bigger formats come each week: the two-voice audio, the explainer video or the one picture. The letter always goes out, and the spoken version and the one-minute video come with most issues. If the format that leads your email is missing, you get the full letter with one line saying so.

What happens when a rule changes after I have written about it?

Your readers see it as one story, not a second surprise. A story that moves stays one story: LongLens marks it as new, an update or a reversal, and links back to the earlier issues that covered it.

Can LongLens show my readers where their field is heading?

In part. A section shows what keeps coming up across your last few weeks of issues. You switch it on in your settings, and it appears once a few issues have gone out.

Can LongLens write my own company news?

No. LongLens works from what the press, the forums and the videos in your field say. Your own announcements are still yours to write, and you can add them as a note in an issue.

Day to day

How do I change what I get?

Reply to any issue in plain words: shorter, more on one topic, drop a website. LongLens confirms what it understood in the same thread, and the next issue follows it. Reply UNDO to take it back. The days and the time are changed on your settings page after you sign in.

Can I stop it for a while?

Yes. Press Pause this newsletter at the bottom of any issue. The same page starts it again.

Can a colleague change my issue by replying?

No. LongLens acts only on replies from the address your issue goes to. A forward to a colleague changes nothing.

What if my issue is late?

If an issue has not gone out an hour after its usual time, LongLens emails you once to say so. You do not need to do anything: it goes out as soon as it is ready, or, if something went wrong while making it, we have already been told.

Money and ownership

What does LongLens cost?

Nothing during the pilot. After it, Solo is $19 a month and Business is $95 a month. See what each plan includes.

Who owns what LongLens writes?

You do. Every issue, every website you chose, every lesson you taught LongLens.
