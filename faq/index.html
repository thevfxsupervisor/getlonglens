FAQ

Straight answers. Including what LongLens does not do yet.

Getting started, signing in, what you get, day to day, price and ownership.

Getting started

How do we start, and how does LongLens reach my customers?

A short call, then Geoff points LongLens at the websites, forums, videos and newsletters you trust, and you describe your customers in your own words. You see a week of real issues before anyone else does. Today each issue comes to you first, and you send it through the email tool you already use, or forward it. Email straight to your customers from your own address is coming.

Can I sign up on my own?

Not yet. LongLens is invite only for now, and Geoff sets up every account himself. Book a short call, or ask for a Free or Solo invite on the pricing page.

How do I sign in?

With a link by email. There is no password. Type your email on the sign-in page and a fresh link arrives, usually within a minute. Each link works once and keeps you signed in for 30 days on that device.

What you get

Is AI writing it?

Yes. LongLens writes with AI, checks names and figures against the original story, tries every link before the issue goes out, and you change anything by replying. Your name is on it, so the last word is yours.

Does LongLens make a separate video for every customer?

No. LongLens crafts each piece once per issue, whether ten people read it or ten thousand. So when each customer picks their own format (coming), the cost still does not grow with your list.

What if an audio, video or slides piece is missing?

Each week you get up to two of the full audio, the video and the slides. Once those two are used, audio still comes as the plainer spoken version. If the piece that leads your email is missing, you get the full letter with one line saying so.

Can LongLens write my own company news?

No. LongLens works from what the trade press, the forums and the videos say about your trade. Your own announcements are still yours to write.

Day to day

How do I change what I get?

Reply to any issue in plain words: shorter, more on one topic, drop a website, add the slides. LongLens confirms what it understood in the same thread, and the next issue follows it. Reply UNDO to take it back. The days and the time are changed on your settings page after you sign in.

Can I stop it for a while?

Yes. Press Pause this newsletter at the bottom of any issue. The same page starts it again.

Can a colleague change my issue by replying?

No. LongLens acts only on replies from the address your issue goes to. A forward to a colleague changes nothing.

Money and ownership

What does LongLens cost?

Free, Solo at $19 a month, or Business at $95 a month. For Business, Geoff quotes the setup after your call, before anything starts. Compare the plans.

Who owns what LongLens makes?

You do. Every issue, every website you chose, every lesson you taught LongLens.

Who is behind LongLens?

Geoffrey Hancock, a VFX supervisor and producer who won a VES Award for Changeling. His own morning VFX briefing, The VFX Supervisor Daily, is crafted with LongLens.
