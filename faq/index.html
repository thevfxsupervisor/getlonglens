Getting started

How do we start, and how does LongLens reach my readers?

Send the form and LongLens emails you a one-time start link at once. Click it, answer three short questions (what LongLens should follow, where and when it arrives, and where it starts looking), and your first issue arrives on the schedule you pick. You see a week of real issues before anyone else does. Today each issue comes to you first, and you send it through the email tool you already use, or forward it. Email straight to your readers from your own address is coming.

Can I sign up on my own?

Yes, from the form. Start your free week and a one-time start link arrives by email at once. Geoff still reads every message himself.

How do I sign in?

With a link by email. There is no password. Type your email on the sign-in page and a fresh link arrives, usually within a minute. Each link works once and keeps you signed in for 30 days on that device.

Trust and control

Does anything reach my clients before I see it?

No. Nothing goes to your clients until you approve it. Every issue comes to you first: you read it, change any line, and decide what to send and to whom.

What if LongLens gets something wrong?

You read every issue before your readers do, and you can change or cut any line. LongLens checks names and figures against the original story and links every story to it, so each fact can be checked in one click. Your name is on it, so the last word is yours.

Is AI writing it?

Yes. LongLens writes with AI, checks names and figures against the original story, and checks links before the issue goes out. You change anything on the page or by replying.

Can I edit the issue myself?

Yes. Open the issue, press Edit in place, click any line and type, then Save. Every version is kept. This works on daily newsletters; a weekly newsletter has its own editor, section by section.

Your clients' details

Does LongLens need my client list?

Not today. Issues come to you, and you send them from the email tool you already use, so your client list stays where it is.

Can LongLens follow news from my country?

Yes. LongLens follows the websites, feeds and forums you name, wherever they are: a tax authority's news page, an industry association, a regulator.

What you get

Is LongLens only for one kind of business?

No. LongLens works for any business whose readers need to know what is changing: an accountancy, a vet practice, a law firm, a recruiter, a consultancy, a school or a members' association. Geoff built it first for his own field, visual effects, and that is the example on this site. Yours follows the websites, forums and newsletters of your field.

What if the audio, slides or videos are missing?

Not every issue gets every format. During the pilot, up to two of the bigger formats come each week: the two-voice audio, the slides, the explainer video or the one picture. The letter always goes out, and the spoken version and the one-minute video come with most issues. If the format that leads your email is missing, you get the full letter with one line saying so.

Can LongLens write my own company news?

No. LongLens works from what the press, the forums and the videos in your field say. Your own announcements are still yours to write, and you can add them as a note in an issue.

Day to day

How do I change what I get?

Reply to any issue in plain words: shorter, more on one topic, drop a website, add the slides. LongLens confirms what it understood in the same thread, and the next issue follows it. Reply UNDO to take it back. The days and the time are changed on your settings page after you sign in.

Can I stop it for a while?

Yes. Press Pause this newsletter at the bottom of any issue. The same page starts it again.

Can a colleague change my issue by replying?

No. LongLens acts only on replies from the address your issue goes to. A forward to a colleague changes nothing.

Money and ownership

What does LongLens cost?

Nothing during the pilot. After it, Solo is $19 a month and Business is $95 a month. See what each plan includes.

Who owns what LongLens writes?

You do. Every issue, every website you chose, every lesson you taught LongLens.

Who is behind LongLens?
