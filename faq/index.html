Getting started

What is LongLens?

LongLens is an AI newsletter service for businesses. LongLens finds what changed in your field and tells your clients what it means, under your name only. The Email, Podcast, Reel, Infographic, Carousel and Explainer video are all part of the issue. Every issue comes to you first, and you make it yours on the page before you send it.

How soon can my clients start hearing from me?

Your first issue is with you in about 5 minutes, written from the websites and topics you gave. You read a week of real issues before anyone else does. Then add your existing clients with a single tick, and LongLens sends them each issue you sign off, with only your name on it and a one-click opt-out in every email. We switch sending on with you when you start.

Can I sign up on my own?

Yes, from the form. Start your free week. Your first issue arrives by email in about 5 minutes. On a very busy day you get a start link instead: click it and answer three short questions.

How do I sign in?

With a link by email. There is no password. Type your email on the sign-in page and a fresh link arrives. Each link works once and keeps you signed in for 30 days on that device.

Trust and control

Is this just my news feeds pasted into an email?

No. LongLens judges each story against what you said your readers care about, and leaves out what does not fit. What stays is retold in plain words, under your name, with a line on why it matters to your readers, not pasted from the original. The same story from two outlets appears only once. Every name, title and number links to the original article. Every story links to its original, one click away, and every issue comes to you first.

Does anything reach my clients before I see it?

No. Nothing goes to your clients until you approve it. Every issue comes to you by email first: you read it, change any line, and decide what to send and to whom. If your issues also go up on a web page, like a blog, each one waits for your OK there too, unless you choose a setting that puts it up for you: Automatic, or Publish at noon without my take (if you have not added your own comment by 12:00, it goes up without it). Both are off until you choose them, you are emailed when an issue goes up, and what goes up also reaches your Slack or Discord channel and your feed.

What if LongLens gets something wrong?

Every issue comes to you first, and you can change or cut any line before you send it. If your issues also go on a web page, you choose: each one goes up on its own, or when you put it up. LongLens links every story to its original, so you or your client can follow any fact in one click. Your name is on it, so the last word is yours.

Whose name is on what my clients read, and will it look like my brand?

Yours, and only yours, so it looks like your firm, not a template. On every plan, every issue carries your name, logo and colours, never ours. Business adds a custom design of your letters, pages and videos, built with you when you start.

Who is responsible for record keeping and professional rules?

Your firm is. You decide what your clients are told, and you keep the records your own professional rules require. When LongLens sends for you, it keeps the proof of who was added as a client and who stopped.

Will AI-drafted issues sound like me?

AI drafts each issue and links every story to its original. Every issue comes to you first, and you make it yours: type on the page to change a line, or reply to the issue email to change the next issue. So it sounds like you.

Can I make every line sound like me?

Yes. Open the issue from the link in your email, press Review and approve, then Edit in place, click any line and type, then Save. Every version is kept. This works on daily newsletters; a weekly newsletter has its own editor, section by section.

Your clients' details

Does LongLens need my client list?

Only if you want LongLens to send for you. Add your existing clients with a single tick, and LongLens sends them each issue you sign off, with only your name on it and a one-click opt-out in every email. Each letter to a client also shows your business name and postal address. We switch sending on with you when you start. Or keep your list where it is and forward each issue yourself.

What happens to my clients' details?

Your clients' names and email addresses are used for one thing: sending them your updates. They never go to an AI. Your replies shape your own newsletter and nobody else's, and your content is never used to teach an AI.

Can LongLens follow news from my country?

Yes. LongLens follows the websites, feeds and forums you name, wherever they are: a tax authority's news page, an industry association, a regulator.

What you get

What does "issue" mean?

One day's or one week's newsletter: the stories LongLens picked, each with what it means for your readers. The Email, Podcast, Reel, Infographic, Carousel and Explainer video are all part of the issue.

Is LongLens only for one kind of business?

No. LongLens works for any business whose readers need to know what is changing: an accountancy, a vet practice, a law firm, a recruiter, a consultancy, a school or a members' association. Geoff built it first for his own field, visual effects, and that is the example on this site. Yours follows the websites, forums and newsletters of your field.

Is the Podcast voice AI?

Yes. Every Podcast is spoken by AI voices, never a person: two AI hosts talking the issue through, or one AI voice reading it.

What happens when a rule changes after I have written about it?

Your readers see it as one story, not a second surprise. A story that moves stays one story: LongLens watches for updates and reversals and links back to earlier coverage.

Can LongLens show my readers where their field is heading?

After your first few issues, a section shows what keeps coming up across your last few weeks of issues.

Can LongLens write my own company news?

No. LongLens works from what the press, the forums and the videos in your field say. Your own announcements are still yours to write, and you can add them as a note in an issue.

Day to day

How do I change what I get?

Hit Reply on any issue in your inbox and write what you want, like "shorter", "more on VAT" or "drop this website". LongLens emails back in the same thread with what it changed, and the next issue follows it. Reply with the word UNDO to take it back. Issues arrive on weekdays at 07:00 your time to start; change the days and the time on your settings page after you sign in. Every issue ends with a Stop button. If you never tap a link, reply or sign in for five issues, LongLens asks once whether you still want them and waits for your yes.

Can I stop it for a while?

Yes. Press Pause this newsletter at the bottom of any issue. The same page starts it again.

If I forward an issue to a colleague, can they change it?

No. Only an email from the address your issues go to changes anything. A colleague who writes back to a forwarded issue changes nothing.

What if my issue is late?

If an issue has not gone out an hour after its usual time, LongLens emails you once to say so. You do not need to do anything: it goes out as soon as it is ready, or, if something went wrong while making it, we have already been told.

Money and ownership

What does LongLens cost?

Free for a week with no card, then from $19 a month. Solo is $19 a month and Business is $95 a month, in US dollars, paid by invoice. See what each plan includes.

Is there a limit during the free week?

Yes: one newsletter, with as many sources as you like and up to 25 readers on your list.

Who owns what LongLens writes?

You do. Every issue, every website you chose, every lesson you taught LongLens.
