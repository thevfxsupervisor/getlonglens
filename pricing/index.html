How the pilot works

No card form. One short form starts your free week.

  • Start from the form. Your first issue arrives by email in about 10 minutes.
  • One click to keep it. Press Keep it coming at the top of that first issue. Then it comes on weekdays at 07:00 your time, and you can change what it covers and when on one page.
  • A monthly cap on writing. If a newsletter reaches it, LongLens says so on your pages, and new issues start again the next month.
  • Up to two of the bigger formats a week: the two-voice audio, the explainer video or the one picture. The letter, the spoken version and the one-minute video carry on as usual.
  • Stop any time. Tell Geoff, and nothing is owed.
