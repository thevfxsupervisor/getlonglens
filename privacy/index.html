No cookies on this site. Your email reaches one person.

This website, getlonglens.com, uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It sets no cookies and does not follow you to other sites. The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages.

When you write to Geoff

Your email goes to Geoffrey Hancock at geoff@wanglemedia.com. LongLens uses it to make your account and email you your newsletter, and Geoff uses it only to reply to you. Writing does not add you to any list.

Once you use LongLens

We notice if you tap a link in your issues, only so we stop emailing anyone who never reads them. Nothing else is read from your issues, and it is not used for anything else.

What the LongLens app keeps about you, where it lives and who else sees anything is set out on the app's own privacy page: LongLens app privacy.
