Late payments cost more from 16 November, and MTD and Budget dates are closing in

HMRC's late payment interest rises to 8% from 16 November, so clients with tax or VAT arrears should pay or arrange time to pay now. MTD quarterly updates are due today for the first group, and the Budget sets April 2027 changes to plan around.

So what: Debt is getting dearer. Interest is higher from mid-November, and VAT late payment penalties already rose for periods from 1 April 2025. Chase clients with unpaid balances now, and set up time to pay arrangements before the penalty windows close.
