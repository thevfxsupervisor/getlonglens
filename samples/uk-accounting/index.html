Client Update

Tue 17 Nov 2026

Tax interest rises to 8% on 24 November, and April 2027 changes are closing in

HMRC's late payment interest goes up to 8% on 24 November after the Bank of England raised its rate to 4%, so clients with tax to pay should pay now or ask for time to pay. Self Assessment is due on 31 January 2027, scammers are using fake refund emails, and several April 2027 changes affect company cars, staff benefits and R&D claims.

Late payment interest goes up to 8%

So what: If you owe HMRC tax or VAT, each month of delay costs more from 24 November: 8% a year, up from 7.75%. Pay what you owe now, or ask us to set up a time to pay plan before the rate bites. Money HMRC owes you earns 3%.
  • Rates and allowances: HMRC interest rates for late and early payments  HMRC (GOV.UK)
    From 24 November 2026 the late payment rate on income tax, VAT, capital gains tax and more is 8.00%, and the repayment rate is 3.00%.

Why: the Bank of England raised its rate

So what: HMRC's interest follows the Bank of England's rate, so this is why the line above moved. It also means a business loan, overdraft or tracker mortgage may cost more, and the Bank expects prices to keep rising into next year. Check what your own borrowing is linked to.
  • Bank rate increased to 4% - November 2026 Monetary Policy Summary and Minutes  Bank of England
    At its meeting ending 4 November the Bank raised its rate by 0.25 points to 4%, and expects inflation to reach around 5% by the middle of next year.

Check both tax accounts before 31 January

So what: If you joined Making Tax Digital for income tax this April, your tax bill may be split across two online accounts. Look at both, and pay everything due by 31 January 2027, because interest and possible penalties start the day after. Agents do not get a copy of HMRC's letter to you, so tell us if you are unsure.
  • MTD taxpayers encouraged to check tax payments  ICAEW
    HMRC is writing to people who joined in April 2026; the balancing payment for 2025/26 and the first payment on account for 2026/27 are both due by 31 January 2027.

Fake tax refund emails are everywhere

So what: HMRC will never email, text or phone to say you are due a refund, or ask you to claim one. If a message says otherwise, do not click, and forward it to us if you are unsure. One August scam sent a fake GOV.UK page to steal bank details, and about 900 people reported it.
  • Nearly 2,000 Self Assessment scams reported  HMRC news (GOV.UK)
    HMRC received 1,704 reports of Self Assessment scams in the 12 months to 31 October 2026, ahead of the 31 January 2027 deadline.

Buying an electric car? Timing matters

So what: If your company or business plans to buy an electric car, buying before 1 April 2027 (6 April if you are a sole trader) means all the cost can be claimed against tax in the first year. Buy after that and the claim is spread over many years at 12% a year, so the same relief arrives much more slowly.
  • 100% first year allowance for electric cars to end in April 2027  ICAEW
    The 100% first year allowance ends for spending on or after 1 April 2027 (company tax) or 6 April 2027 (income tax); the yearly rate for these cars drops from 14% to 12%.

Company cars and private medical cover go on the payroll

So what: If you give staff a company car, a company van or private medical insurance, from 6 April 2027 the tax on those benefits has to be collected through each month's payroll, instead of in a form after the year ends. Check your payroll software can do this, and tell us if you have staff living or working abroad.
  • Mandatory payrolling of benefits now less than five months away  ICAEW
    From 6 April 2027 employers must process company cars and fuel, vans and fuel, and medical benefits through payroll; most other benefits follow from April 2028.

Research and development: one form fewer

So what: If your company develops new products or methods and may claim R&D tax relief, the advance notice some companies must give HMRC is scrapped from 1 April 2027. Until then, missing the six month deadline can cost you the relief, so tell us early if you have a claim in mind.
  • Businesses should not lose R&D tax relief over administrative oversight  ATT
    The Budget removes the R&D claim notification form from 1 April 2027; today some companies must notify HMRC within six months of the end of the accounting period.

Companies House: file company accounts by software from April 2028

So what: Nothing to do this year. From April 2028 the Companies House website and paper forms close for filing annual accounts, so every company's accounts must go in through accounting software. If we prepare your accounts, we will handle it. If you do them yourself, plan to switch.
  • Companies House update to accounts filing from April 2028  Companies House (GOV.UK)
    All UK companies must file accounts in iXBRL format using commercial software from April 2028, while the plan to make small companies file profit and loss accounts is paused.
