Your content stays yours. Stop any time.

These terms cover LongLens, the newsletter service at getlonglens.com, run by Hedge Holding ApS. They are short on purpose. Last updated 12 November 2026.

Your first week is free

Starting costs nothing and needs no card. After your free week you pay only if you pick a paid plan; plans and prices are on the pricing page, invoiced in US dollars. LongLens is new and still changing, so we may limit how many new newsletters start each day.

Your content and your readers stay yours

Your topics, the websites you name, your readers' email addresses and the issues you send are yours. LongLens uses them to write and deliver your newsletter, as the privacy page sets out, and for nothing else. We never sell your readers' addresses or add them to any list. Every story links to its original, and the original belongs to its publisher.

Your name, your call

LongLens writes each issue with AI, and every issue comes to you first. You make it yours and you decide what goes out under your name, so what you send is your responsibility.

No guarantees

LongLens is provided as it is. We work to keep it running and its stories right, but we cannot promise it is free of mistakes or always available. As far as Danish law allows, we are not liable for any loss that comes from using it.

Stop any time

You can stop whenever you like: press Stop at the end of any issue, or email geoff@wanglemedia.com. We can stop your newsletter too, and when we do we tell you by email first whenever we can.

Changes and the law

We may change these terms as LongLens changes. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we email you about any change that affects you. Danish law applies, and the Danish courts decide any dispute.
